    Questions

    Do you come to me?

    Yes. SUHO Detailing is mobile: we come to your location. We travel up to 35 miles from downtown Houston. To check your address, type it into the address check in the service area section.

    How much does a detail cost?

    Auto Refresh is $65 and Motorcycle Refresh is $50. These are introductory prices. Additional charges may apply for heavily soiled vehicles.

    What does the Auto Refresh include?

    Basic interior + exterior: interior vacuum and surface wipe-down, interior glass and basic exterior wash.

    Do you detail motorcycles?

    Yes. Motorcycle Refresh, $50, is a basic wash + detail: bodywork and accessible surfaces cleaned, wheels, seat and finishing wipe-down.

    How do I book?

    Pick a service and an open time on our Square booking page, or text or call us. Text or call (346) 698-3058 with your vehicle, your location and a time that works, or send a DM on Instagram at @suhodetailing.
